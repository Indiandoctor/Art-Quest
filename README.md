# Art Quest

A family trivia game that introduces children to 100 of the world's most influential artworks: paintings, sculpture, buildings and ancient art from Europe, India, Africa, Asia and the Americas.

**Play:** https://indiandoctor.github.io/Art-Quest/

- 100 artworks in Easy (1 pt), Medium (2 pts) and Hard (3 pts) levels, mixed or played in order
- Three clues before each guess: the artist and year, where the work is now, and a "Did you know?" fact
- Take-turns or buzzer-race mode for up to 8 players, with high scores saved in the browser
- After each answer, photos of the artist, the place and the fun fact; an art book to browse every work
- Works offline once loaded: everything is in one `index.html`

Pictures come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons under their free licences; all artworks in this version are in the public domain.
